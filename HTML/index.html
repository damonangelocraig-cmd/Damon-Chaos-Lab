What is Chaos?

Chaos Theory is the study of unpredictable, nonlinear systems and their possible outcomes according to certain initial conditions. In other words, it’s the prediction of a possible outcome in a system that is constantly and chaotically changing. Today, we use Chaos Theory to model various real-world mathematical systems: weather/climate forecasting, economic markets (Fractal Market Hypothesis), cardiology, population dynamics, engineering, and SO MUCH MORE!!!
